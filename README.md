# Quetza-balanza
A place to collect the things I tried and the mistakes that take me one step closer to understanding.
Or maybe to the place where I accept what I can't.

# What this Repo really holds
The Quetza-balanza repo will house the things I was interested in trying that are typically outside of my level of expertise. There will be many different areas of knowledge that my projects will fit in and I'll try to keep them organized based on the goal of what I'll learn. 
For example...
## Tech
The projects in this category will typically aim to build a skill in a certain programing/scripting language, automation, server set-up, ect.
## Writing
Projects in this category will typically showcase my understanding of research documents through summarized writings, my own creative writing, or my way of expressing ideas in both professional and casual ways. Being able to convey complex ideas to those not of the expertise, industry, or understanding is crucial to both professional and casual environments. Exacting and expressing critical thinking is a skill that is learned and not inherited.
